Safety & Preparedness Tips

Simple steps you can take today to become better prepared.

📋 Make a Plan

Talk with your family about what you will do during an emergency.

📱 Stay Informed

Learn how to receive emergency alerts and warnings in your area.

🎒 Prepare Supplies

Keep an emergency kit somewhere that is easy to access.

🚪 Know Your Exits

Learn the safest exits from your home, school, or workplace.

👨‍👩‍👧 Help Others

Check on neighbors and family members who may need additional assistance.

🔋 Check Your Supplies

Regularly check your emergency supplies and replace expired or damaged items.
