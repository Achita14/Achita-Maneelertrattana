General Preparedness

📋 Make a Plan

Establish emergency meeting points and designate an out-of-town relative as a single point of contact for family members.

📱 Stay Informed

Sign up for local weather alerts, download emergency apps, and keep a battery-powered or hand-crank radio nearby.

🎒 Prepare Supplies

Build a 72-hour kit containing water (1 gal/person/day), non-perishable food, flashlights, and a first-aid kit in an accessible spot.

📁 Secure Documents

Keep physical copies of insurance policies, medical records, and IDs in a waterproof bag or encrypted USB drive.

Home Safety & Utilities

🔧 Utility Shut-Offs

Learn where your main gas, water, and electrical shut-off valves are located and how to operate them safely.

🚪 Know Your Exits

Map at least two exit routes from every room in your home, workplace, or school, and practice family evacuation drills.

🧯 Fire Safety

Test smoke and carbon monoxide detectors monthly. Keep an ABC-rated fire extinguisher on every level of your home.

🔋 Maintain Supplies

Rotate emergency food and water every six months. Test battery-powered equipment regularly and replace old batteries.

Event-Specific Actions

🧱 Earthquakes

Drop, Cover, and Hold On. Stay away from windows, outer walls, and heavy items that could fall.

🌊 Floods

Move to higher ground immediately. Never drive or walk through flooded roadways—"Turn Around, Don't Drown."

🔥 Storms & Fires

Clear flammable vegetation within 30 feet of your house. Follow local official orders promptly during evacuation warnings.

👨‍👩‍👧 Community Support

Check in on elderly neighbors, individuals with mobility needs, and families with pets to ensure everyone stays safe.

In Case of Immediate Danger

If you or someone around you requires immediate medical or life-saving assistance, call local emergency services immediately.
