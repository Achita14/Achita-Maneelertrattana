📞 Emergency Contacts in Thailand

Keep these important emergency numbers available in case you or someone around you needs help.

Emergency Service Phone Number When to Call
🚔 Police Emergency 191 Crime, accidents, or immediate danger
🚑 Medical Emergency 1669 Serious injury, illness, or ambulance
🔥 Fire & Rescue 199 Fire, rescue, or other fire emergencies
🌪️ National Disaster Warning 192 Disaster warnings and emergency information
👮 Tourist Police 1155 Help and assistance for tourists
🚗 Highway Police 1193 Emergencies on highways
🌊 Water Emergency 1196 Water-related accidents or emergencies
🛂 Immigration Police 1178 Immigration and visa-related assistance
