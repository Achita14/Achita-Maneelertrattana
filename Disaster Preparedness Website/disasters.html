Types of Disasters

Learn about the different disasters that can affect communities.

🌋

Natural Disasters

Events caused by natural processes.

🌪️

Weather Disasters

Dangerous events caused by severe weather.

🏭

Human-Made

Disasters caused by human activity.

🌪️ Typhoon / Hurricane

Powerful tropical storms with strong winds and heavy rain.

Warning Signs

Weather forecasts, dark clouds, strong winds, heavy rain.

Typical Warning Time

Days to a week.

What to Do

Follow weather warnings and prepare to shelter or evacuate.

🌊 Flood

Flooding occurs when water covers land that is normally dry.

Warning Signs

Continuous heavy rain, rising rivers, flood warnings.

Typical Warning Time

Hours to days.

What to Do

Move to higher ground and follow official instructions.

🌋 Volcanic Eruption

An eruption occurs when magma, ash, and gases escape from a volcano.

Warning Signs

Small earthquakes, gas emissions, ground swelling.

Typical Warning Time

Days to months.

What to Do

Follow evacuation orders and avoid dangerous volcanic areas.

🌊 Tsunami

A series of powerful ocean waves usually caused by an underwater earthquake.

Warning Signs

Strong coastal earthquake, sudden sea withdrawal, tsunami alerts.

Typical Warning Time

Minutes to hours.

What to Do

Move immediately to higher ground or inland after a strong coastal earthquake.

❄️ Blizzard

A severe winter storm with heavy snow, strong winds, and very low visibility.

Warning Signs

Weather forecasts, dropping temperatures.

Typical Warning Time

Days.

What to Do

Stay indoors, keep warm, and follow weather warnings.

🌵 Drought

A long period of unusually low rainfall that causes water shortages.

Warning Signs

Long periods of little rainfall.

Typical Warning Time

Weeks to months.

What to Do

Conserve water and follow local water-use guidelines.

🔥 Wildfire

An uncontrolled fire that spreads through vegetation and other areas.

Warning Signs

Smoke, dry weather, fire weather warnings.

Typical Warning Time

Hours to days.

What to Do

Follow evacuation orders and leave immediately when instructed.

🌎 Earthquake

Sudden shaking of the ground caused by movement in Earth's crust.

Warning Signs

Usually no reliable warning signs. Small foreshocks may occur, but they are not dependable.

Typical Warning Time

Seconds with early warning systems, or none.

What to Do

Drop, Cover, and Hold On.

The Most Dangerous and Common Disasters in Thailand

Disasters can be dangerous when someone does not know how to handle them.

🌊 1.Floods

The most frequent, especially during the rainy season. Heavy monsoon rainfall and overflowing rivers can cause widespread flooding.

🌪️ 2.Tropical storms / tropical cyclones

Thailand is affected by tropical storms and remnants of typhoons, bringing strong winds and heavy rainfall.

🌍 3.Droughts

Periods of insufficient rainfall can cause water shortages, crop damage, and agricultural problems, particularly in northeastern and central Thailand.

Floods

Floods are dangerous in Thailand because heavy monsoon rains, tropical storms, and low-lying areas can cause large amounts of water to accumulate quickly. Flooding can have a major impact on daily life by damaging homes, roads, vehicles, and other property. It can also make transportation difficult, causing people to be late for work or school, while severe floods may force schools and businesses to close. In addition, floodwater can contaminate drinking water and increase the risk of diseases and injuries. Therefore, floods can seriously disrupt people's daily activities and cause significant damage to communities.

Tropical Storms & Tropical Cyclones

Tropical storms and tropical cyclones can affect Thailand, especially during the rainy season from May to October. Most systems develop over the western Pacific Ocean or the South China Sea and move westward toward mainland Southeast Asia; by the time they reach Thailand, they are often weaker tropical depressions or storms. However, they can still bring very heavy rainfall, strong winds, flooding, landslides, and rough seas. The effects are often greatest in northern and northeastern Thailand, while southern Thailand can experience heavy rain at different times because of the northeast monsoon. Tropical cyclones are most common in the region around July to October, and their rainfall can significantly increase Thailand’s flood risk.

Droughts

A drought is a long period of unusually low rainfall that causes a shortage of water. Droughts can happen when there is little or no rain for many months, high temperatures increase water loss, or climate change affects normal weather patterns. They can have serious effects on people, animals, and the environment. Droughts may cause crops to fail, reduce drinking-water supplies, dry up rivers and lakes, and increase the risk of wildfires. Farmers are often affected the most because they depend on water for growing crops and raising animals. Conserving water, improving irrigation systems, planting drought-resistant crops, and protecting forests can help reduce the effects of droughts.
