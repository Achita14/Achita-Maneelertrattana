Disaster Warning Signs

Learn what different warnings and warning signs can mean and how you should respond.

🔴 Warning

A warning generally means a dangerous event is happening or expected soon. Take appropriate action immediately.

🟠 Watch

A watch generally means conditions are favorable for a dangerous event. Stay informed and be ready to act.

🟡 Advisory

An advisory generally indicates a situation that may cause inconvenience or hazards and requires caution.

What Should You Do?

  1. Pay attention to official alerts.
  2. Read the instructions carefully.
  3. Prepare to evacuate if instructed.
  4. Check on family members and neighbors.
  5. Continue monitoring official information.

Remember

Warning systems can be different depending on the disaster and location. Always follow instructions from local authorities and emergency services.
